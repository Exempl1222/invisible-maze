Gnome Sweet Gnome

a fence-building puzzle

A garden gnome has run away and is heading home by the shortest path. Build wooden fences on the lines between tiles so the walk is too long for him. Use as few fences as you can.

How a level works

  • The gnome

    He always heads home by the shortest path, and he only has so many steps in him. Stumps count as one step.

  • Your fences

    Build them on the lines between tiles, all before he sets off. Make his walk longer than his move limit, but always leave him a way through. Par is the fewest fences that work.

  • The garden

    Rivers and rocks stop him. Bridges and hollow stumps let him through. You can't fence a line next to the gnome, his house, a bridge or a stump.
