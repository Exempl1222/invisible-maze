How a level works

  • The robot

    It always takes the shortest route to the beacon, and it has a move limit. Teleporters count as one step.

  • Your walls

    Draw them on the lines between tiles, all before it moves. Cut every route, or make the shortest one too long. Par tells you the fewest walls that work.

  • The terrain

    Rivers and rocks block the robot. Bridges and teleporters let it through. A guard rule keeps you honest: you can't wall a line next to the robot, the beacon, a bridge or a teleporter.
