Gnome Sweet Gnome

a fence-building puzzle

The garden, all together

One gnome crosses the bridge facing right. The other walks down, hits a freshly built wall and shrugs.

Walls build in the order they were placed, so a burst of taps reads as a row of fences going up.

Four directions, three moves

Twelve states in total. Left is the right-facing art flipped, which is standard for sprite sets and keeps both sides identical.

Wooden walls

Each one loops here so you can watch the full build. In the game it plays once, the moment the player places a wall.

Hollow stump portals

The gnome hops up, drops into the hollow, and pops out of the twin stump in a flash of light. Each pair shares a glow colour so players can match them at a glance.

Teleport, 6 second loop: hop in, flash, flash, pop out, hop down.

Tile kit

The fixed pieces of each level, drawn on a 64 unit grid.

Style rules for the builder

  • Dark brown outline on every shape, never black. Thicker on the gnome than on tiles.
  • Two tones per surface: base colour plus one shade on the lower right. One small highlight, top left.
  • Rounded, slightly lumpy shapes. Nothing perfectly geometric.
  • Strong red is kept for the hat and the goal. Glow colours only ever mean magic.
  • Facing left is always the right-facing sprite flipped.
